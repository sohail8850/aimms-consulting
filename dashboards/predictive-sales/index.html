Key insights

  • Discounts cost money, not buy volume: units per sale stay ≈10 at every discount level, but revenue per sale falls ~30% from the 0–5% to the 25–30% band.
  • No trend or seasonality: annual revenue oscillates around $0.8M and no month stands out, so the outlook is flat (~$2.2K/day).
  • Categories are balanced (~25% each); store performance ranges only ±10%, so gains come from pricing/discount policy, not mix.
  • Customer rating has no link to revenue — treat it as a service KPI, not a sales driver.

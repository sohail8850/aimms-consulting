Launch Communications Audit: Verifying National Campaign Messaging

A national product launch campaign was graded line by line before its media roll-out. Most headline claims tested as Affect-Driven or Plausible-Unverified rather than Verified.

The challenge

The campaign was about to reach national media. Its headline claims sounded confident, but confidence is not evidence. Superlatives such as "most revolutionary" and "unmatched" gave competitors and regulators something to challenge, and gave the brand nothing it could prove.

How we approached it

Using Semantic BI, every claim in the campaign was read for what it actually asserts and graded into one of three categories.

Affect-Driven

Creates a feeling or impression but states nothing that can be tested.

Plausible-Unverified

Sounds believable, but no evidence on hand supports it.

Verified

Falsifiable, and backed by evidence that can be shown on request.

Each claim that fell short was rebuilt into falsifiable language, with a named evidence source behind it.

Claims rebuilt

"The most revolutionary clinical asset in targeted therapy."

Affect-Driven

"Demonstrates a 3.4x higher target binding affinity in Phase III trials compared to standard-of-care baseline."

Verification basis: Peer-reviewed Phase III trial data, published biomarker assays, clear comparator baseline.

"Provides unmatched supply chain reliability for nationwide retail distribution."

Plausible-Unverified

"Maintains a 99.4% on-time fulfillment rate across 45 regional distribution centers over 12 consecutive months."

Verification basis: Historical ERP fulfillment logs, audited regional logistics reports, 12-month trailing data.

"Engineered with pure, industry-leading eco-friendly manufacturing."

Affect-Driven

"Manufactured in facilities powered by 100% renewable energy, reducing Scope 1 and 2 carbon emissions by 42% per unit."

Verification basis: ISO 14064 third-party carbon audit, facility utility certification reports.

Outcome

Three core claims rebuilt into falsifiable, defensible language ahead of launch, surviving both internal legal review and external scrutiny.

Legal and regulatory protection

100% of rewritten headline claims passed internal legal compliance on the first review pass, with no ad-copy disclosures or restrictive disclaimers needed.

Risk elimination

Unsubstantiated superlatives and greenwashing exposure were removed, closing the openings that competitors and regulators could have used.

Market differentiation

Generic marketing rhetoric gave way to verified performance metrics across paid, owned, and earned channels.

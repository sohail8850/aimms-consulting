The Brand

Setting the Scene

Concor, a beta-blocker (Bisoprolol Fumarate), was heading into launch at Merck Pharmaceutical Pakistan (now Martin Dow Marker) with a promotional plan already built, printed, and rehearsed. The plan had one problem: it was built on a clinical misread that no one in the room was willing to name out loud.

01 The Strategic Dilemma

A Beta-Blocker Aimed at the Wrong Target

Marketing had identified what looked like white space: Pregnancy-Induced Hypertension (PIH), a segment dominated by Aldomet (Methyldopa), a centrally-acting alpha-2 agonist. The plan was to position Concor as the modern alternative.

The flaw sat underneath the strategy slide, not on it: Concor is a beta-blocker, and beta-blockers are largely contraindicated in pregnancy-induced hypertension. The team had picked a rival correctly on paper and a battlefield incorrectly in practice.

02 The Feedback Gap

Groupthink in Action

By the time feedback was requested, the promotional materials were already printed and the launch narrative was already set. Asking for validation at that stage rewards agreement, not accuracy and that is exactly what happened: 19 of 20 Regional Sales Managers returned feedback confirming the plan, without independently checking the clinical reality with prescribers on the ground.

Root Cause

Fear of the Marketing Head, combined with a fixed-mindset culture, converted a field-validation exercise into a rubber stamp.

03 The Turning Point

Evidence-Based Dissent

One Regional Sales Manager, based in Lahore, did what a validation exercise is supposed to involve: he went and checked. He visited Lady Wellington Hospital and secured a formal letter from the Professor of Gynecology confirming that beta-blockers carry real risk in pregnancy hypertension fetal bradycardia and growth restriction among them.

05 The Pivot

From Near-Failure to Cardiovascular Dominance

Once the correct clinical lens was applied, the fix was straightforward: retarget the chronic hypertension and heart failure market, reposition against the true beta-blocker competitors, and rebuild the promotional narrative from that foundation.

Outcome

Concor grew into a PKR 3 billion brand by 2024, with 2025 projected at PKR 3.25 billion a launch that nearly failed on a clinical misread became a cardiovascular category leader.

Key Issues & Strategic Solutions

Viewed through the AIMMS lens, this case is a textbook example of how launch strategy fails silently not through bad intentions, but through validated assumptions that no one re-checks once momentum builds. Below is the issue-to-solution mapping AIMMS would apply to prevent a repeat.

Issue "Gold Plated Higher Management"

Senior sign-off was treated as clinical validation. A Marketing Head's confidence in the plan substituted for evidence that was never actually gathered.

Solution Evidence-Grading Discipline

Every claim behind a launch strategy should be graded Verified, Plausible-Unverified, or Affect-Driven-Unfalsifiable before it reaches promotional material. A "contraindication check" against actual prescriber input would have surfaced this in week one, not after printing.

Issue Fake Feedback as a Structural Risk

19 of 20 field validators returned agreement, not information. The feedback loop was compromised by fear before it ever reached the MD.

Solution Pre-Mortem Warning Constellation

Running a structured pre-mortem asking each RSM to argue why the launch could fail, anonymously and before materials are finalized breaks the social pressure to conform and surfaces dissent while it is still cheap to act on.

Issue Misidentified Competitive Set

Aldomet was named the rival on the strength of category adjacency, not mechanism-of-action logic a beta-blocker was benchmarked against a non-beta-blocker.

Solution Mariana Trench-Level Market Research

Competitive mapping needs to go beneath the therapeutic category to the pharmacological mechanism and the contraindication profile the level of depth that would have named Inderal and Tenormin as the real competitive set from day one.

Issue No Psychological Safety for Dissent

The one RSM who did the work almost didn't get heard the system depended on individual courage rather than a process built to surface inconvenient evidence.

Solution Institutionalized Dissent Channel

Psychological safety shouldn't rely on one person's courage. A standing "red-team" review where at least one RSM is formally mandated to challenge the plan with field evidence before every major launch makes this case study's hero-moment a routine safeguard instead of a lucky accident.

06 Key Lessons

For Management

Intellectual Honesty

Psychological safety has to be built in a subordinate must be able to challenge a superior with data, without professional risk.

Evidence Over Emotion

In pharma, a single clinical letter outweighs a full deck of promotional material. Data settles the argument that opinion cannot.

Decisive Leadership

A leader has to be willing to burn the sunk cost and pivot the moment the strategy is shown to be fundamentally flawed.

Case Studies / The 3 Billion Milestone
Cardiovascular Segment (Formerly Merck Marker Pakistan — Now Martin Dow Limited)

Strategic dominance without a price war

How Concor (Bisoprolol Fumarate) turned a mature, 50-competitor molecule into a record-breaking brand by competing on clinical truth instead of discount.

PKR 3.0B
REVENUE MILESTONE, 2024
50+
LOCAL GENERIC COMPETITORS
3
STRENGTHS 2.5 / 5 / 10 MG
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Maturity, turned into dominance

By 2024, Concor was a mature molecule facing intense pressure from over fifty local generic competitors. Rather than engaging in a destructive price war or succumbing to macroeconomic headwinds, a combination of strategic business intelligence, scientific leadership, and supply chain resilience propelled the brand to an unprecedented PKR 3.0 billion milestone.

THE CHALLENGE

A mature brand in a crowded market

The Business Unit Head and commercial leadership faced three primary hurdles in a highly competitive cardiovascular category.

Price sensitivity

Local generics such as Barilol and Corbis entered the market priced 50–60% below multinational pricing.

Economic headwinds

Severe inflation across Pakistan and complex Letter of Credit restrictions threatened raw material (API) imports industry-wide.

Market share retention

Defending "Top of Mind" awareness among cardiologists who were being aggressively courted by cheaper alternatives.

THE BUH STRATEGY

Four pillars of the defense

Rather than compete on price, MDM elevated the brand above generic commoditization through a deliberate, multi-pronged strategy.

SCIENTIFIC LEADERSHIP

Evidence-based medicine, not price

Sponsored cardiology symposiums and engaged key institutions, reinforcing Concor's superior bioavailability and pharmacokinetic consistency over unbranded alternatives.

PORTFOLIO DEPTH

The full strength spectrum

From the widely used 2.5mg titration dose to the 5mg and 10mg maintenance strengths, precise therapy for hypertension and advanced heart failure comorbidities locked in long-term patient compliance.

SUPPLY CHAIN

Reliability as a clinical asset

While local competitors struggled with raw material stock-outs under macroeconomic restrictions, MDM's supply chain kept Concor continuously available. To a physician prescribing a cardiac drug, that reliability is priceless.

DIGITAL PRESENCE

A culture of trust and authority

Professional digital platforms were used to celebrate clinical milestones and share peer-to-peer success stories, building market authority with both practitioners and field teams.

THE RESULT

Unchallenged market leadership

By refusing to race to the bottom on price and instead competing on clinical truth, supply reliability, and complete portfolio depth including the vital 2.5mg strength Concor cemented its legacy. In 2024, the brand crossed the monumental PKR 3.0 billion revenue mark.

"Premium quality, backed by field intelligence, always wins in the long run."

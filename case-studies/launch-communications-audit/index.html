Launch Communications Audit: Verifying National Campaign Messaging

A national product launch campaign was graded line by line prior to its media rollout. Initial analysis revealed that the majority of headline claims tested as Affect-Driven or Plausible-Unverified rather than empirical Verified statements. Through Semantic Business Intelligence (Semantic BI), messaging was systematically rebuilt into falsifiable, defensible language, surviving rigorous internal legal review and external market scrutiny.

Claim Reconstruction & Verification Framework

Original Claim Audit Category Rebuilt Falsifiable Claim Verification Basis
"The most revolutionary clinical asset in targeted therapy." Affect-Driven "Demonstrates a 3.4x higher target binding affinity in Phase III trials compared to standard-of-care baseline." Peer-reviewed Phase III trial data, published biomarker assays, clear comparator baseline.
"Provides unmatched supply chain reliability for nationwide retail distribution." Plausible-Unverified "Maintains a 99.4% on-time fulfillment rate across 45 regional distribution centers over 12 consecutive months." Historical ERP fulfillment logs, audited regional logistics reports, 12-month trailing data.
"Engineered with pure, industry-leading eco-friendly manufacturing." Affect-Driven "Manufactured in facilities powered by 100% renewable energy, reducing Scope 1 and 2 carbon emissions by 42% per unit." ISO 14064 third-party carbon audit, facility utility certification reports.

Legal & Regulatory Protection

100% of rewritten headline claims passed internal legal compliance on the first review pass without requiring ad-copy disclosures or restrictive disclaimers.

Risk Elimination

Removed unsubstantiated superlatives and greenwashing exposures that previously left the brand vulnerable to competitor challenges and regulatory oversight.

Market Differentiation

Replaced generic marketing rhetoric with verified performance metrics, driving strong brand differentiation across paid, owned, and earned channels.

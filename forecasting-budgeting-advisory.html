Why budgets miss

Most forecast misses are decisions, not arithmetic.

A spreadsheet can be perfectly built and still carry five assumptions nobody tested. These are the ones we find most often.

Stock read as demand Distributor loading lifts primary sales and then reverses. The budget books it as growth.
Price hides volume Revenue grows because prices rose while units fell. Without a price, volume and mix split, nobody sees it.
A price rise on a fixed date Approval timing is treated as certain, with the pricing framework itself under government review.
One exchange rate Imported API cost is modelled at a single rupee level, with no lag for stock already on the shelf.
Targets nobody believes Numbers handed down from head office reach the territory as a quota to survive, not a plan to own.

Every number in your budget gets one of three grades
Verified Traced to a source document or system record. Safe for the base case.
Plausible-Unverified Reasonable but not yet confirmed. Carried with a range, not a point.
Affect-Driven-Unfalsifiable Held by conviction and impossible to test. Kept out of the base case.

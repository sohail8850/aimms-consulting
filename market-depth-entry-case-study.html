Reading Market Depth Before Committing Capital

A category-entry case study: what sat beneath the growth headline

Entry depth: −1,200 m (category assumption)
Surfaced at: Challenger Deep
Market ResearchSemantic BIStrategic Advisory
Finding

Category growth was real, but it lived in one crowded sub-indication. The safer route in was a narrower, less-contested indication first, then the wider category.

Situation

A commercial team was preparing to enter a new therapeutic category. The internal business case rested mainly on category-level growth figures, the kind of number that looks the same from the surface whichever part of the market produced it.

Anonymised engagement. Client details are withheld and the case is described at the level of method and decision, not company data. The point is the way of reading a market, which applies to any category-entry decision.

The descent

A trench-level pass went below the category headline. It used three layers of evidence and asked one question of each: where is the growth actually coming from, and who is already there?

Three depths, one question

−1,200 m · CATEGORY ASSUMPTION

The business case: category-level growth, read as an average that applies everywhere in the category.

MID-DESCENT · THREE LAYERS READ

IQVIA MAT data, prescriber concentration by tier, and channel margin structure, read together.

CHALLENGER DEEP · SURFACED

Growth sat in a single sub-indication that most competitors were already crowding.

Illustrative gauge. Depth labels are the AIMMS convention for how far below the headline a reading goes.

What each layer was asked

No single layer would have changed the decision on its own. The concentration only became visible when the three were laid side by side.

LayerQuestion askedWhat it showed
IQVIA MAT dataSplit the moving annual total by sub-indication. Which segments produce the category's growth, and which are flat?Category growth was concentrated in one sub-indication, not spread across the category.
Prescriber concentration by tierWhich prescriber tiers drive volume in that sub-indication, and how many competitor teams already call on them?The high-value prescriber tiers were already heavily contested by established competitors.
Channel margin structureWhat does each channel keep, and does the margin hold for a new entrant at the volumes needed?Entering the crowded segment head-on meant competing for the same prescribers and channel terms as incumbents.

Findings are stated qualitatively because the engagement is anonymised. In a live engagement each row carries its own figures and sources.

Two ways to enter

The category headline invited a full-category entry. The layered reading pointed to a different first move.

What it buys
Scale on paper

A large addressable number, but a share that has to be won from entrenched competitors.

Volume and trust

Early volume and prescriber trust, built before the wider push.

Surfaced with

A recommendation to enter through a narrower, less-contested indication first, building volume and prescriber trust before a full-category push.

  1. PHASE 1

    Enter through the narrower indication. Build volume, prescriber relationships and field credibility where competitors are fewer.

  2. PHASE 2

    Use that base as evidence. Prescriber trust and real-world volume make the wider case to the same and adjacent prescriber tiers.

  3. PHASE 3

    Decide on the full-category push from observed results, not from the opening growth headline.

What a category-entry team should take from it

  1. Treat category-level growth as a starting question, not a business case. Split it by sub-indication before it carries any weight.
  2. Ask where the growth is concentrated and how many competitors are already calling on the prescribers who generate it.
  3. Read market data, prescriber tiers and channel margins together. Each alone can mislead.
  4. Prefer a first indication where a new entrant can be heard, and let early results fund the larger decision.
  5. Set the review point in advance: what must be true after phase 1 before phase 2 is released?
Scope. Commercial analysis based on the data layers described. Validate figures against your own licensed data and confirm regulatory and promotional constraints with qualified counsel in each market.

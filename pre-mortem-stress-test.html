Pre-Mortem Stress Test: Realigning Ambition with Performance

A pre-mortem on a pharmaceutical group's growth strategy found a shortfall that was not a market failure. It was an alignment failure between ambition and the execution engine behind it.

The challenge: a 7 billion PKR altitude gap

The group had declared an ambitious destination, but its trajectory stayed close to the ground. Sales kept growing, yet 2025 was heading well below its potential.

Value-wise sales, PKR billions. The 7 billion gap between the 2025 estimate and its potential was an alignment failure, not a market failure.

What the pre-mortem found

Motion mistaken for progressAbout 30 me-too product launches between late 2024 and 2025 created operational noise that drowned out value. More launches diluted focus and fragmented the field force's attention.
Narrative driftBrand milestones were celebrated loudly. They were real achievements, but they became a narrative shield that hid the structural stagnation of the total portfolio.
Execution fatigueWith 30+ launches to carry, the field force's messaging was diluted and selling turned mechanical.

Mapping the structural risks

Risks were placed on an impact and likelihood matrix so leadership could see where to act first.

Q1 · CRITICAL

Cultural rigidity, tech debt

Q2 · STRUCTURAL LEADERSHIP

Gold-plated higher management

Senior leaders accumulating privilege and symbolic authority without delivering equivalent strategic value, widening the gap between head office and market reality.

Q3 · OPERATIONAL

Process drift

Q4 · EMERGING

Regulatory shifts

The response: four pillars

The answer was predictive strategic foresight: anticipating failure paths and mapping risks before they materialise, so Business Intelligence stops reporting the past and starts rehearsing the future.

1. Strategic ResetRecalibrate the narrative

Move from counting launches and milestone posts to portfolio contribution mix, category share gains and incremental revenue per launch. Growth must be structural, not just optical.

2. Portfolio Re-EngineeringRationalise the me-too strategy

Prune low-traction SKUs, halt the 33 undifferentiated launches planned for 2026, and reallocate resources to 3–4 differentiated anchor categories. The shift is from a volume-based launch strategy to a value-based portfolio strategy.

3. Execution TransformationRebuild the engine

Replace activity monitoring with capability coaching, and prioritise brands by territory. A focused team sells; a fatigued team merely distributes.

4. Foresight and GovernanceThe intelligence system

A repeating cycle of risk detection, monthly pre-mortem review, strategic intervention and execution monitoring, with the critique of gold-plated leadership behaviours made part of the institution.

The 2026–2027 blueprint

A stepped path back to altitude, from about 48 billion PKR in 2025:

2026 · STABILISATION

52–54 billion PKR

Portfolio pruning and launch excellence.

2027 · ACCELERATION

58–62 billion PKR

Category leadership and pipeline activation.

The ambition is earned through stabilisation first, then acceleration. These figures are the plan's targets, not reported results.

The leadership mandate

The destination is not something to declare; it is something to prepare for.

  • Reward truth over optics.
  • Dismantle gold-plated privileges.
  • Build a culture of strategic honesty.
